## Problem

What cannot be done today, in plain words. Include the evidence you have — a number, a complaint, a
support volume, a thing that broke. Not a solution.

## Proposed outcome

What is true once this is done, from the user's point of view. Still not an implementation.

## Affected users and systems

Who feels this, and which parts of the app or infrastructure it touches.

## Constraints

What the solution must respect: platforms, privacy, an existing contract, a store policy, a
deadline, a minimum OS version. This is the section that is most often skipped and most often
regretted.

## Out of scope

What this deliberately does not cover, so the work does not quietly grow.

## Open questions

What is genuinely undecided. Leave them here rather than guessing; an unanswered question is
cheaper than a wrong assumption.
